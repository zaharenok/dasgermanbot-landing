# 7 Netflix Shows That Will Teach You German Better Than Any Textbook

Textbooks teach you to say "Der Hund ist groß." Netflix teaches you how Germans actually talk — fast, mumbled, full of slang. Here are seven shows ranked from easiest to hardest, and what you'll pick up from each.

## 1. Extra auf Deutsch (A1-A2)

This is a cheesy sitcom designed for learners. Slow dialogue, clear pronunciation, repetitive vocabulary. It feels like a 90s soap opera because it is one.

**What you learn:** Basic greetings, daily routines, simple questions. The acting is bad, but the German is perfectly paced for beginners.

## 2. Dark (A2-B1)

Germany's most famous Netflix export. A small town, missing children, time travel. Everyone whispers. The dialogue is slow and atmospheric.

**What you learn:** Formal German, family vocabulary, past tense. The show uses a lot of "wir müssen reden" (we need to talk) energy. Turn on German subtitles — without them you'll miss half the plot anyway.

## 3. How to Sell Drugs Online (Fast) (B1)

Three teenagers run a drug empire from a bedroom. It's funny, fast, and full of youth slang.

**What you learn:** Teen slang, internet vocabulary, colloquial grammar. You'll hear "Krass!", "Echt jetzt?", "Alter!" constantly. This is how 20-somethings in Germany actually speak.

## 4. Babylon Berlin (B1-B2)

Weimar Republic, 1929. Detectives, cabaret, political intrigue. Beautifully shot, complex plot.

**What you learn:** Formal address, workplace German, historical vocabulary. The characters switch between formal "Sie" and informal "du" depending on status — you'll see the boundary in action.

## 5. Biohackers (B1-B2)

A medical student uncovers a conspiracy. Fast-paced, science-heavy, set in Freiburg.

**What you learn:** Academic German, university vocabulary, medical terms. Great for anyone planning to study in Germany.

## 6. Türkisch für Anfänger (B1-B2)

A German-Turkish blended family navigates culture clash. One of Germany's most popular comedy series.

**What you learn:** Family vocabulary, cultural references, humor. The show switches between German and Turkish — you'll hear real multicultural German as spoken in Berlin and Cologne.

## 7. Tetris (B2-C1)

Not purely German, but large portions are in German. The story of the video game rights, set during the Cold War.

**What you learn:** Business negotiation German, political vocabulary, fast-paced dialogue between native speakers. If you can follow this without subtitles, you're ready for real life.

## How to actually learn from Netflix

Watching passively teaches you nothing. Do this instead:

1. **German audio + German subtitles.** Not English. You need to see the words.
2. **Pause and repeat.** Hear a phrase? Stop. Say it out loud. Three times.
3. **Write down three words per episode.** Not fifty. Three. Look them up. Use them.
4. **Re-watch episodes.** Your brain catches more the second time. The first time is plot. The second time is language.
