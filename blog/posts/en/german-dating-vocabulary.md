### Pickup Lines (Cheesy but Effective)

| German Phrase | English Translation | Context |

### Natural Conversation Starters

**At a bar/cafe:**
> "Hi, ich heiße [Name]. Darf ich mich setzen?"
> *(Hi, my name is [Name]. May I sit here?)*

**At a party:**
> "Hi, wie kennst du den Gastgeber?"
> *(Hi, how do you know the host?)*

**Online dating opener:**
> "Hallo! Dein Profil hat mich neugierig gemacht. Was machst du gerne in deiner Freizeit?"
> *(Hello! Your profile made me curious. What do you like to do in your free time?)*

## Cultural Context: German Dating Norms

### What's Different About Dating in Germany?

**Direct Communication:**
- Germans are straightforward about intentions
- No playing games or "hard to get"
- Clear communication is valued

**Paying the Bill:**
- "Wer zahlt?" (Who pays?) — Often discussed openly
- Splitting the bill ("Getrennt bezahlen, bitte") is common
- Traditional gender roles are changing

**Punctuality:**
- Being on time shows respect
- Lateness without notice is disrespectful
- 5-10 minutes late is acceptable with notice

**Physical Contact:**
- Consent is important ("Darf ich dich küssen?")
- Move slower than some other cultures
- Public displays of affection (PDA) are common but tasteful

**Progression:**
- "Fremdschämen" (stranger shame) — public PDA limits
- Relationships progress more slowly but seriously
- "Sich kennenlernen" takes time
