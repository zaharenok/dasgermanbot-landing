# German at the Doctor: Survival Phrases for Arzt and Apotheke

You're sick. Your throat burns. You need a doctor. The receptionist answers the phone in rapid German. Panic sets in. Here's everything you need to survive the German healthcare system without crying.

## Booking an appointment

German doctors have a specific word for appointment: **Termin**. Use it.

- **Ich möchte einen Termin vereinbaren.** — I'd like to make an appointment.
- **Haben Sie heute noch einen Termin frei?** — Do you have an appointment free today?
- **Es ist dringend.** — It's urgent.
- **Ich bin neu hier. Brauche ich eine Überweisung?** — I'm new here. Do I need a referral?

If you have public insurance (**gesetzliche Krankenversicherung**), you can go to any doctor. If private (**private Krankenversicherung**), some doctors prioritize you. Check when booking.

## At the doctor's office

You'll wait. A lot. Bring a book. When they finally call your name:

- **Was fehlt Ihnen?** — What seems to be the problem? (This is what the doctor asks you.)
- **Ich habe...** — I have...

Then describe what's wrong:

- **...Kopfschmerzen.** — ...a headache.
- **...Halsschmerzen.** — ...a sore throat.
- **...Fieber.** — ...a fever.
- **...Bauchschmerzen.** — ...a stomach ache.
- **...Hustenschutz.** — ...a cough.
- **...eine Erkältung.** — ...a cold.
- **...Rückenschmerzen.** — ...back pain.

More detail:

- **Seit wann haben Sie diese Beschwerden?** — Since when have you had these symptoms?
- **Seit drei Tagen.** — For three days.
- **Ich bin allergisch gegen...** — I'm allergic to...
- **Ich nehme folgende Medikamente:** — I take the following medications:

## Understanding your diagnosis

The doctor will explain something quickly. Key words to catch:

- **Entzündung** — Inflammation
- **Infektion** — Infection
- **Verschreibung** — Prescription
- **Rezept** — Prescription (same thing, more casual)
- **Überweisung** — Referral to a specialist
- **Krankschreibung** — Sick note (you need this for your employer)
- **Apotheke** — Pharmacy (where you pick up medicine)

**Wichtig:** If you need a sick note for work, ask: **"Können Sie mich krankschreiben?"** (Can you write me a sick note?) This is legally required in Germany.

## At the pharmacy

German pharmacies (**Apotheken**) are not drugstores. They only sell medicine and health products. The green cross on the door is your sign.

- **Ich habe ein Rezept.** — I have a prescription.
- **Haben Sie etwas gegen Kopfschmerzen?** — Do you have something for headaches?
- **Gibt es das auch als Generika?** — Is there a generic version? (Cheaper, same effect)
- **Wie oft soll ich das nehmen?** — How often should I take this?
- **Gibt es Nebenwirkungen?** — Are there side effects?

Over-the-counter basics:
- **Schmerzmittel** — Painkillers
- **Hustensaft** — Cough syrup
- **Fieberthermometer** — Thermometer
- **Pflaster** — Band-aids

## Emergency: 112

If it's serious — chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing — call **112**. The operator speaks English.

- **Ich brauche einen Krankenwagen.** — I need an ambulance.
- **Die Adresse ist...** — The address is...
