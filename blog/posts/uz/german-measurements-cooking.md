## The Metric System in Germany

**Important:** Germany uses the **metric system** exclusively. No cups, no tablespoons, no Fahrenheit!

### Basic German Measurement Units

#### Weight Units (Gewichtseinheiten)

| German | English | Abbreviation | Equivalent |
|--------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Milligramm** | Milligram | mg | 0.001g |
| **Gramm** | Gram | g | Basic unit |
| **Kilogramm** | Kilogram | kg | 1000g |
| **Tonne** | Ton | t | 1000kg |

**Usage examples:**
- "100 Gramm Mehl" = "100 grams of flour"
- "Ein Kilo Äpfel" = "A kilo of apples"
- "Eine Tonne" (hyperbole) = "A ton" (a lot)

#### Volume Units (Hohmaße)

| German | English | Abbreviation | Equivalent |
|--------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Milliliter** | Milliliter | ml | 0.001L |
| **Centiliter** | Centiliter | cl | 0.01L |
| **Deziliter** | Deciliter | dl | 0.1L |
| **Liter** | Liter | l | Basic unit |

**Usage examples:**
- "250 Milliliter Milch" = "250 ml of milk"
- "Ein halber Liter" = "Half a liter"
- "Ein Viertelliter Wein" = "A quarter liter of wine"

#### Length Units (Längenmaße) — for baking

| German | English | Abbreviation | Equivalent |
|--------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Millimeter** | Millimeter | mm | 0.001m |
| **Zentimeter** | Centimeter | cm | 0.01m |
| **Meter** | Meter | m | Basic unit |

**Usage in baking:**
- "Eine Form von 20 Zentimetern" = "A 20cm form"
- "Ein Zentimeter hoch" = "One centimeter high"

## Recipe Vocabulary: Rezeptwörter

### Reading German Recipes

**Typical German recipe structure:**

```markdown
# Schokoladenkuchen (Chocolate Cake)

**Zutaten (Ingredients):**
- 200 Gramm Mehl (200g flour)
- 100 Gramm Zucker (100g sugar)
- 3 Eier (3 eggs)
- 100 Milliliter Milch (100ml milk)

**Zubereitung (Instructions):**
1. Den Ofen auf 180 Grad vorheizen (Preheat oven to 180°)
2. Die Zutaten verrühren (Mix ingredients)
3. Den Teig in die Form füllen (Fill batter into form)
4. 45 Minuten backen (Bake 45 minutes)

**Backzeit (Baking time):** 45 Minuten
**Ofentemperatur (Oven temperature):** 180°C (Umluft)
```

## Cooking Techniques: Gartechniken

### Common Cooking Methods

1. **Kochen (Boiling)** — Cooking in boiling water
   - Example: "Kartoffeln kochen" (Boil potatoes)
   - Temperature: 100°C

2. **Dämpfen (Steaming)** — Cooking with steam
   - Example: "Gemüse dämpfen" (Steam vegetables)
   - Healthier than boiling

3. **Dünsten (Sautéing)** — Cooking in little fat
   - Example: "Zwiebeln dünsten" (Sauté onions)
   - Low fat method

4. **Braten (Frying/Roasting)** — Cooking with fat
   - Example: "Schnitzel braten" (Fry schnitzel)
   - Pan: Pfanne, Oven: Ofen

5. **Grillen (Grilling)** — Cooking over open heat
   - Example: "Würstchen grillen" (Grill sausages)
   - BBQ season: Grillsaison

6. **Backen (Baking)** — Cooking in oven
   - Example: "Kuchen backen" (Bake cake)
   - Sweet and savory dishes

7. **Schmoren (Braising)** — Slow cooking with liquid
   - Example: "Gulasch schmoren" (Braise goulash)
   - Tenderizes tough meat

## Practice Exercise: Try This Recipe!

### Simple German Pancakes (Pfannkuchen)

**Zutaten (Ingredients):**
- 100 Gramm Mehl (100g flour)
- 2 Eier (2 eggs)
- 200 Milliliter Milch (200ml milk)
- 1 Prise Salz (pinch of salt)
- Butter zum Braten (butter for frying)

**Zubereitung (Instructions):**
1. Das Mehl mit einer Prise Salz in eine Schüssel geben (Put flour with salt in bowl)
2. Die Eier und die Milch dazugeben und alles gut verrühren (Add eggs and milk, mix well)
3. Den Teig 10 Minuten ruhen lassen (Let batter rest 10 minutes)
4. Eine Pfanne erhitzen und etwas Butter hineingeben (Heat pan, add butter)
5. Einen Teil des Teigs in die Pfanne geben (Pour some batter in pan)
6. Von beiden Seiten goldbraun backen (Brown on both sides)
7. Mit Apfelmus oder Zucker servieren (Serve with applesauce or sugar)

**Guten Appetit!** (Enjoy your meal!)
