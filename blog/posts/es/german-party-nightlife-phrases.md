# German Party and Nightlife Phrases: Surviving Berlin Clubs and Beyond

Germany takes partying seriously. Berlin clubs open on Friday and close on Monday. Bavarian beer gardens serve litres at a time. Whatever your scene, here's how to survive a night out auf Deutsch.

## Ordering drinks

Skip the menu. Walk up to the bar and order:

- **Ein Bier, bitte.** — One beer, please.
- **Ein Helles.** — A light lager (Bavarian standard).
- **Ein Dunkles.** — A dark beer.
- **Ein Weißbier.** — A wheat beer.
- **Eine Maß Bier.** — A litre of beer (Oktoberfest essential).
- **Einen Shot, bitte.** — A shot, please.
- **Noch eine Runde.** — Another round.
- **Ich zahle.** — I'm paying. (Bold move. Respect.)

To ask for the bill: **"Zahlen, bitte."** Or **"Getrennt, bitte"** if you're paying separately — Germans always split the bill unless someone specifically offers.

## At the beer garden

Beer gardens are chaos with rules. Know them:

- **Selbstbedienung** — Self-service. You get your own beer and food. Look for the counter.
- **Pfand** — Deposit on your glass. Return it to get your money back.
- **Brotzeit** — A snack plate (bread, cheese, cold cuts). The perfect beer companion.
- **Prost!** — Cheers! Say it while making eye contact. Always make eye contact — not doing so is seven years of bad sex, according to German superstition.

## Talking to people

German party small talk is blunt and direct:

- **Woher kommst du?** — Where are you from?
- **Was machst du beruflich?** — What do you do for work?
- **Wie gefällt dir die Party?** — How do you like the party?
- **Lass uns tanzen!** — Let's dance!
- **Kommst du oft hierher?** — Do you come here often? (Yes, Germans use this too.)
- **Willst du was trinken?** — Do you want a drink?

If someone buys you a drink, the rule is: you buy the next round. **"Die nächste Runde gehe ich."** (The next round is on me.)

## The Berlin club experience

Berlin bouncers are legendary. Especially at Berghain. Tips for getting in:

- Wear black. Or at least don't look like a tourist.
- Don't take photos. Ever.
- Don't be loud in the line.
- When the bouncer asks **"Wie viele?"** (How many?), answer with a number. Not a sentence. Just **"Zwei"** or **"Drei."**
- If they stare at you silently, stay calm. They're deciding. Don't talk.

Key club vocabulary:

- **Einlass** — Entrance
- **Kasse** — Ticket counter / cash register
- **Garderobe** — Coat check
- **Bühne** — Stage
- **Dancefloor** — Yes, they use the English word

## When it's time to leave

- **Ich muss gehen.** — I have to go.
- **War nett mit dir!** — It was nice meeting you!
- **Lass uns in Kontakt bleiben.** — Let's stay in touch.
- **Wir sehen uns.** — See you around.

And the next morning:
- **Ich habe Kater.** — I have a hangover.
- **Bruder, was ist passiert?** — Bro, what happened?

## Numbers you'll need

At the bar, at the door, at the coat check — numbers come fast. Know yours:
- **zwei** (2), **drei** (3), **vier** (4), **fünf** (5), **sechs** (6), **sieben** (7), **acht** (8), **neun** (9), **zehn** (10)
