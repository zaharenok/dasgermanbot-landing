## Usage Tips

### When to Use Slang

✅ **Use slang when:**
- With friends and peers
- In casual social settings
- On social media
- In informal text messages
- With people your age or younger
- In relaxed work environments (sometimes)

❌ **Avoid slang when:**
- In formal writing
- With authority figures
- In job interviews
- With elderly people
- In academic contexts
- In customer service

### Tone and Context

- **Geil** can be inappropriate with older people
- **Alter** is very casual, use with friends
- **Krass** is generally safe for youth
- **Na?** is versatile but casual
- **Tschüss** is polite but informal
- **Du** vs **Sie** matters greatly
