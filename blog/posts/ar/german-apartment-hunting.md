# German Apartment Hunting: How to Find a Wohnung Without Losing Your Mind

Finding an apartment in Germany is a competitive sport. In Berlin or Munich, 50 people show up to viewings for a single flat. You need to be fast, prepared, and speak enough German to not get filtered out immediately.

## Reading the listing

German rental ads have their own language. Decode it:

- **Kaltmiete (KM)** — Cold rent (base rent, no utilities). This is NOT what you pay.
- **Warmmiete (WM)** — Warm rent (base + utilities like heating, water, garbage). This is closer to reality.
- **Nebenkosten (NK)** — Additional costs (utilities). Usually €150-€350 extra.
- **Kaution** — Deposit. Usually 2-3 months of Kaltmiete.
- **Abstand** — Key money / payment to previous tenant for their furniture. Legally grey. Common in big cities.
- ** Provision** — Agent fee. If the ad says " provisionsfrei " — no fee. Always look for this.
- **Einbauküche (EBK)** — Built-in kitchen. Crucial detail — many German flats come WITHOUT a kitchen. Literally no counters, no sink, no stove.

## Other key listing terms

- **WBS** — Wohnberechtigungsschein. A certificate for subsidized housing. You need it for cheap flats.
- **BJ** — Bezugsfrei ab sofort. Available immediately.
- **EG** — Erdgeschoss. Ground floor.
- **DG** — Dachgeschoss. Attic/top floor. Can be roasting in summer.
- **HH** — Hinterhof. Courtyard building. Quieter but darker.
- **saniert** — Renovated. **teilsaniert** — Partially renovated (the landlord painted one wall).
- **Altbau** — Old building (pre-WWII, high ceilings, beautiful, drafty).
- **Neubau** — New building. Modern, efficient, expensive.

## Writing your application

In Germany, you don't just view a flat. You apply for it. Landlords want a **Mieter-Selbstauskunft** — a self-disclosure form with your income, job, and personal info. Some also want a **Schufa-Auskunft** (credit check).

Your email needs to stand out. Use this:

```
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

ich bin an der Wohnung in der [Street] sehr interessiert.

Zu meiner Person: Ich bin [Age] Jahre alt, arbeite als [Job] bei [Company]
und habe ein festes Einkommen. Ich bin zuverlässig, ruhig und rauche nicht.

Eine Schufa-Auskunft und Mieter-Selbstauskunft sende ich gerne auf Wunsch.

Wann wäre ein Besichtigungstermin möglich?

Mit freundlichen Grüßen
[Name]
[Phone]
```

## At the viewing

- **Wann ist der Mietbeginn?** — When does the lease start?
- **Sind Haustiere erlaubt?** — Are pets allowed?
- **Gibt es einen Keller?** — Is there a basement?
- **Ist ein Stellplatz verfügbar?** — Is a parking spot available?
- **Wie hoch sind die Nebenkosten?** — How much are the utilities?
- **Wie lange ist die Kündigungsfrist?** — What's the notice period?

## Understanding the contract

- **Mietvertrag** — Rental contract. Read every word.
- **Kündigungsfrist** — Notice period (usually 3 months).
- **Staffelmiete** — Rent increases by a fixed amount each year.
- **Indexmiete** — Rent tied to inflation.
- **Kaufvertrag** — Not a rental — they're selling. Run.

## Pro tips

1. Apply within 30 minutes of seeing a listing. Speed matters.
2. Bring your Schufa, Mieter-Selbstauskunft, and last 3 payslips to every viewing.
3. Dress like you're going to a job interview. Landlords judge.
4. Mention you're quiet and don't smoke. These are the magic words.
