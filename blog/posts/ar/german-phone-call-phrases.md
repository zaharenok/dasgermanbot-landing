# German Phone Call Phrases: How to Survive Calling Anyone in Germany

Your phone rings. Unknown number. German voice. Panic. Here's every phrase to sound like you belong — and what to say when you have no idea what they just said.

## Answering the phone

Germans state their last name when picking up. You should too.

- **[Your last name], hallo.** — Standard answer.
- **Guten Tag, [Last name] am Apparat.** — Good day, [Last name] speaking. (More formal.)
- **Ja, hallo?** — Casual, with friends.

**Never say** just "Hallo?" — it sounds rude in Germany. Say your name.

## Calling someone

- **Guten Tag, mein Name ist [Name].** — Good day, my name is...
- **Ich möchte gerne mit [Name] sprechen.** — I'd like to speak with...
- **Könnte ich bitte mit [Name] verbunden werden?** — Could I please be connected to...?
- **Ist [Name] erreichbar?** — Is [Name] available?

## When you didn't understand

This will happen. Often. Memorize these:

- **Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?** — Could you please repeat that?
- **Können Sie bitte langsamer sprechen?** — Could you speak slower, please?
- **Ich spreche nicht so gut Deutsch.** — I don't speak German very well.
- **Sprechen Sie vielleicht Englisch?** — Do you perhaps speak English?
- **Können wir das auf Englisch besprechen?** — Can we discuss this in English?

## Making appointments by phone

- **Ich möchte gerne einen Termin vereinbaren.** — I'd like to make an appointment.
- **Haben Sie noch Termine frei?** — Do you have any appointments available?
- **Geht es am [Day] um [Time]?** — Does [Day] at [Time] work?
- **Das passt mir leider nicht.** — That doesn't work for me, unfortunately.
- **Passt es Ihnen am...?** — Does ... work for you?

## Leaving a message

- **Kann ich eine Nachricht hinterlassen?** — Can I leave a message?
- **Könnten Sie ihm/ihr ausrichten, dass [Name] angerufen hat?** — Could you tell him/her that [Name] called?
- **Meine Rückrufnummer ist...** — My callback number is...

## Ending the call

- **Vielen Dank, auf Wiederhören.** — Thank you, goodbye. (Formal — use this.)
- **Danke, tschüss!** — Thanks, bye! (Informal.)

**Auf Wiederhören** is the standard phone goodbye. Not "Auf Wiedersehen" — that's for in-person. Germans will notice if you mix these up.

## Automated systems (Die Mailbox)

German voicemail systems are confusing. Key words:

- **Bitte sprechen Sie nach dem Ton.** — Please speak after the tone.
- **Drücken Sie die [number].** — Press [number].
- **Ihre Nachricht wird gespeichert.** — Your message is being saved.
- **Auflegen** — Hang up
- **Zurückrufen** — Call back

## Emergency calls

- **Ich brauche einen Arzt.** — I need a doctor. (Call 112)
- **Ich möchte die Polizei rufen.** — I want to call the police. (Call 110)
- **Es ist ein Notfall.** — It's an emergency.

## Pro tips

- State your last name when answering — always.
- Use **Sie** form on the phone unless you know the person.
- Germans answer work calls fast — no small talk. Get to the point.
- Write down key phrases before important calls. It's not cheating, it's strategy.
