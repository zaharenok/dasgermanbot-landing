# Surviving Deutsche Bahn: German Phrases for Public Transport

German public transport works brilliantly. Until it doesn't. When the screen flashes "Verspätung" and 200 people sigh in unison, you'll want to know what's happening. Here's every phrase you actually need.

## At the ticket machine

Machines take cards, but carry coins. Some regional machines only accept cash — learned this the hard way at 6 AM in Dresden.

- **Eine Fahrkarte nach Berlin, bitte.** — A ticket to Berlin, please.
- **Hin und zurück.** — Round trip.
- **Einfach.** — One way.
- **ICE / IC / RE / S-Bahn** — Train types. ICE is fast and expensive. RE is slow and honest about it.
- **Bahncard?** — Discount card. If you travel twice, buy one.

At the counter: **Ich möchte eine Fahrkarte nach München kaufen.** (I'd like to buy a ticket to Munich.)

## Finding your platform

German stations are logical. Usually.

- **Der Zug nach Köln fährt von Gleis 7.** — The train to Cologne departs from platform 7.
- **Wo ist der Ausgang?** — Where is the exit?
- **Wo ist die Toilette?** — Where is the toilet? (Usually costs 1 euro. Bring coins.)
- **Gleis** — Platform. **Abfahrt** — Departure. **Ankunft** — Arrival.

## On the train

The conductor will say something incomprehensible over the speaker. Every time. Here's what they're probably saying:

- **Nächster Halt: Hannover Hauptbahnhof.** — Next stop: Hanover central station.
- **Bitte zurücktreten.** — Please step back.
- **Die Tür schließt automatisch.** — The door closes automatically.
- **Ihre Fahrkarten bitte.** — Your tickets, please.

You: **Entschuldigung, ist dieser Platz frei?** (Excuse me, is this seat free?)

## When things go wrong

They will. Deutsche Bahn has a 70% on-time rate and a 100% meme rate.

- **Der Zug hat Verspätung.** — The train is delayed.
- **Der Zug fällt aus.** — The train is cancelled.
- **Ersatzfahrt mit dem Bus.** — Replacement by bus. The four saddest words in German.
- **Wie lange Verspätung?** — How long is the delay?
- **Ich brauche eine Bescheinigung.** — I need a delay certificate (for your boss or insurance).

## U-Bahn and Bus

Cities have their own systems. Same logic, smaller scale.

- **Einzelkarte** — Single ticket. **Tageskarte** — Day pass. **Wo steige ich um?** — Where do I transfer?
- **Haltestelle** — Stop. **Nächste Haltestelle: Alexanderplatz.** — Next stop: Alexanderplatz.
- **Drücken Sie den Stop-Knopf.** — Press the stop button.
- **Einsteigen / Aussteigen** — Board / exit.

## Numbers you'll hear

Gleis 3, Bahnsteig 7, Verspätung 45 Minuten. German numbers trip everyone up on platforms because you're processing fast. Practice: *dreiundzwanzig* (23), *vierundfünfzig* (54), *siebenundsechzig* (67).
